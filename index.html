Selected Project Experience

A few products I have led or built at work, and what was hard about each one.

E-commerce | B2B

Vepaar

Business Problem: Small businesses selling over WhatsApp had no simple way to run a catalogue, take orders and keep track of customers.

Technical Challenge: Keeping the app fast for stores with large catalogues and a lot of product images.

Leadership Led mobile engineering for a product used by 100K+ merchants.
Impact A merchant can set up an online store and start taking orders from their phone.
Stack: Flutter, REST APIs, WebSocket
Google Play vepaar.com
Lifestyle | Social

DateDish

Business Problem: Meeting someone from a dating app is risky when you can't tell if the profile is real.

Technical Challenge: Selfie and ID verification, plus a date proposal flow where nothing is confirmed until both people agree.

Architecture Real-time updates over Socket.io for proposals and messages.
Impact Shipped on both Android and iOS.
Stack: Flutter, Node.js, Socket.io
Google Play datedish.com
Media | Entertainment

Live Audio Broadcasting

Business Problem: DJs, musicians and talk hosts wanted to go live in proper audio quality, straight to their followers.

Technical Challenge: A custom player for live and replayed broadcasts that keeps playing in the background and recovers on a bad network.

Strategy Tuned background playback and buffering so streams don't cut out.
Impact Scaled to 50K+ concurrent listeners.
Stack: Flutter, JustAudio, WebRTC
Pharma | Enterprise

Pharma Ecosystem

Business Problem: Distributors, retailers and field salesmen were juggling phone calls, paper orders and a separate ERP.

Technical Challenge: Order booking that works without network and syncs with the ERP once the phone is back online.

Architecture Local SQLite store on the device with a background sync engine.
Impact 30% improvement in distribution efficiency.
Stack: Android (Java/Kotlin), SQLite
Enterprise | Sales

KOOPS

Business Problem: Companies could not see where their field sales team was or which orders were coming in.

Technical Challenge: Reliable GPS tracking through the working day, and order booking that works offline.

Leadership Designed and built the live tracking system.
Impact 40% increase in sales reporting efficiency.
Stack: Android, Kotlin, Google Maps
Google Play koops.in
Networking | Smart Tech

Tapomo

Business Problem: Paper business cards run out, get lost and go out of date.

Technical Challenge: Writing and reading NFC cards, and sharing a profile by tap, QR code or link on both platforms.

Architecture One profile link behind every card, so details can be updated without reprinting.
Impact Live on Android and iOS. Users share as many cards as they like with nothing to print.
Stack: Flutter, NFC Core, Deep Linking
Google Play tapomo.com
